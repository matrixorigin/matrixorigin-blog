MatrixOrigin has been awarded the bid for the Shenzhen Unicom Smart Manufacturing AI Application Project​ and has signed the contract. The project has now entered the development and delivery phase. Together with Shenzhen Unicom, MatrixOrigin will serve a listed electronics manufacturer in Shenzhen, focusing on five business scenarios—precision product recommendation, equipment maintenance, energy management, production insight, and supplier evaluation—to integrate AI capabilities into the manufacturer's daily operations and production management workflows.

## Partnering with Shenzhen Unicom to Bring AI into Manufacturing Operations

Targeting a listed electronics manufacturer in Shenzhen, the project addresses the company's practical needs in product selection, equipment maintenance, energy management, production operations, and supply chain management. It will deliver a suite of AI applications that offer clear business value, support rapid validation, and enable continuous iteration.

Electronics manufacturers typically possess vast amounts of product documentation, equipment manuals, production records, energy consumption data, and supplier information. However, this data is scattered across different systems and documents, resulting in inefficient searches, difficulty preserving institutional knowledge, and lagging data analysis.

MatrixOrigin will collaborate with Shenzhen Unicom on scenario planning, data governance, AI application development, and deployment delivery. This will move AI beyond isolated tools and into real business processes, building intelligent capabilities that are perceivable, analyzable, and traceable for manufacturing enterprises.

## Five AI Application Scenarios Covering Key Manufacturing Business Links

The project focuses on five representative smart manufacturing scenarios, following an iterative, quick-win approach with rapid validation and continuous iteration, to explore viable paths for AI deployment in manufacturing.

### 1. AI-Powered Customer Profiling and Precision Product Recommendation

Addressing challenges such as a large number of product models, complex technical parameters, and heavy reliance on expert experience for selection, the system will structurally interpret customer requirements by combining customer needs, product specifications, and historical selection knowledge.

AI will assist in product parameter retrieval, candidate model recall, specification comparison, and substitution feasibility assessment, while providing recommendation rationale and conflict explanations. This helps sales and technical support teams shorten product selection cycles and improve customer response times.

### 2. AI-Driven Equipment Maintenance Recommendation and Optimization

The system will transform equipment maintenance manuals, inspection records, fault cases, and spare parts data into a searchable, traceable equipment maintenance knowledge base.

When frontline staff input equipment names or fault descriptions, AI can analyze potential causes, recommend inspection steps, repair methods, and safety precautions, and display relevant knowledge sources. This reduces document lookup time and redundant communication costs while promoting the continuous accumulation of maintenance expertise within the enterprise.

### 3. AI-Based Factory Energy Consumption Forecasting and Optimization

Integrating data on factory power usage, production output, shifts, and production units, the system will establish a unified energy consumption analysis view to analyze unit energy consumption, consumption trends, and abnormal fluctuations.

By identifying correlations between energy consumption changes and production activities, the system helps managers detect high-consumption areas, shift variances, and anomalies in production units—providing a data foundation for subsequent energy-saving strategy formulation and fine-grained energy management.

### 4. Intelligent Production Data Visualization and Anomaly Early-Warning

The system will aggregate data from production planning, output reporting, quality inspection, production takt (cycle time), and line-stop records to form a unified production operations view.

Managers can intuitively understand plan attainment rates, product qualification rates, defect rates, takt compliance, and line-stop losses. AI-assisted analysis of primary anomaly causes reduces manual report compilation and cross-system queries, improving the speed of production issue detection and response.

### 5. AI-Enabled Supplier Performance Evaluation and Risk Early-Warning

Addressing issues such as fragmented supplier evaluation data, inconsistent evaluation criteria, and delayed risk detection, the system will build a comprehensive supplier evaluation framework across quality, cost, delivery, service, and technology dimensions.

AI will generate supplier scores, grade distributions, rankings, and watchlists, while explaining low-scoring items and potential risks. This provides references for supplier reviews, procurement decisions, and supply chain risk management.

## Powering Continuous Scenario Iteration with Data + AI Capabilities

Behind these five application scenarios is not merely a single model or Q\&A tool, but a complete capability system spanning data ingestion, knowledge governance, model services, AI agent orchestration, and application delivery.

MatrixOrigin will leverage the MatrixOne Intelligence (MOI)​ data intelligence platform and its agent capabilities to uniformly ingest and govern structured data, business documents, and knowledge assets within the enterprise. By combining knowledge retrieval, data analytics, model inference, and agent workflows, the platform delivers reusable AI capabilities across diverse business scenarios.

During project implementation, both parties will prioritize data inventory and governance, business knowledge base construction, model and agent development, application UI development, system deployment, and pilot validation—continuously optimizing application performance based on feedback from frontline users.

This approach not only reduces the upfront validation cost of AI applications but also establishes a unified data and technical foundation for expanding into more production, operations, and supply chain scenarios in the future.

## Creating a Replicable AI Deployment Model for Manufacturing

Today, manufacturing AI is gradually moving from proof-of-concept into the deep end of real business operations. What truly determines the value of AI applications is not just model capability, but the ability to effectively integrate with enterprise data, business knowledge, and production processes.

This collaboration combines Shenzhen Unicom's strengths in industry services, project organization, and localized delivery with MatrixOrigin's technical expertise in Data + AI platforms, enterprise knowledge bases, agent development, and data applications—jointly exploring a manufacturing AI deployment path that is cost-controlled, delivery-agile, and results-verifiable.

As the project enters the development and delivery phase, MatrixOrigin will maintain close collaboration with Shenzhen Unicom, advancing application development and pilot validation across the five scenarios to truly integrate AI capabilities into enterprise business processes—establishing a referenceable and replicable deployment model for the digital-intelligent transformation of the electronics manufacturing industry.
