# MatrixOrigin Hosts AI Leadership Dinner in Silicon Valley

**From Compute to Intelligence to Productivity**

On the evening of September 21, MatrixOrigin hosted an AI Leadership Dinner in Silicon Valley under the theme “From Compute to Intelligence to Productivity.” The dinner welcomed a delegation of Chinese AI industry companies visiting NVIDIA headquarters and brought industry partners together to explore enterprise AI adoption and opportunities for collaboration.

![Chinese AI industry delegation at NVIDIA headquarters](./images/1.png)

Guests included Yingsong Xia, Chairman of Zhongding; Sugar Tang, COO of HAND; Mile Han, Vice President of iSoftStone; and Chengkun Liu, Vice President of AsiaInfo. Representing MatrixOrigin were Dr. Feng Tian, CTO; Dr. Zuyu Zhang, Scientist; and CK Tan, International Business Partner. They joined guests in discussions on AI infrastructure, enterprise agents, and industry applications.

## Connecting Silicon Valley Technology with Industry Needs

With a core team based in Silicon Valley, MatrixOrigin participated in the visit and hosted the dinner to strengthen connections between the global AI computing ecosystem and the business needs of Chinese enterprises. Through continued technical exchange and collaboration on enterprise deployments, MatrixOrigin aims to help partners bring innovation into practical business use.

![MatrixOrigin AI Leadership Dinner in Silicon Valley](./images/2.jpg)

## Building the Value Chain from Compute to Productivity

The discussions centered on a shared question: how can investment in computing capacity create lasting business value?

MatrixOrigin aims to work with partners across the full value chain: Token Factory delivers efficient, reliable model services; AI Factory connects enterprise data, knowledge, and agents; and Intelligence Factory helps improve business processes and decision-making. Together, these connect computing capacity and AI capabilities to enterprise productivity.

Realizing this vision calls for contributions from partners in computing platforms, infrastructure, models, enterprise software, and industry services. Through open technology and collaboration, MatrixOrigin welcomes partners to build solutions, serve customers, develop markets, and share in the long-term value of enterprise AI.

Meeting in Silicon Valley, with collaboration reaching around the world, MatrixOrigin looks forward to working with more partners on the journey from compute to intelligence to productivity.
